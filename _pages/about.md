I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at the University at Buffalo, and a Presidential Fellow. I am advised by [Ranga Setlur](https://www.buffalo.edu/cubs/members.host.html/content/shared/engineering/computer-science-engineering/profiles/research-scientists-post-docs/setlur-srirangaraj.html) and [Venu Govindaraju](https://cse.buffalo.edu/~govind/). I work as a graduate research assistant in the [Center for Unified Biometrics and Sensors](https://www.buffalo.edu/cubs.html).

My research is in machine learning and computer vision. With collaborators at CUBS I coauthored [ProxyFusion](/projects/proxyfusion/), presented at NeurIPS 2024, which aggregates a set of face features for recognition in unconstrained, long-range imagery. I also work on vision-language models for generating reliable educational material for children, as part of the [AI4ExceptionalEd](https://www.buffalo.edu/ai4exceptionaled/about-us.html) Institute. Earlier at CUBS I worked on detecting and summarizing handwritten content in whiteboard lecture videos. Between those projects I studied neural-network methods for numerical homogenization of multiscale partial differential equations with Matthew Knepley, and presented that work at the SIAM Conference on Computational Science and Engineering in 2023.

I received a BS in Computer Science from the University at Buffalo in 2019.
