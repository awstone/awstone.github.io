2024

  1. NeurIPS
    ProxyFusion: Face Feature Aggregation Through Sparse Experts
    Bhavin Jawade, Alexander Stone, Deen Dayal Mohan, and 3 more authors
    In Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems, 2024
    Abs Bib HTML PDF Code Website

    Face feature fusion is needed for robust recognition when not every frame of a long-range, low-resolution video is equally informative. ProxyFusion selects a sparse set of pooling experts with learnable proxies and aggregates a variable-size set of face features in linear time. The method is order-invariant, does not need intermediate feature maps or extra face metadata, and can run on legacy biometric templates. Evaluations on IARPA BTS3.1 and DroneSURF show gains for unconstrained long-range face recognition.

2021

  1. ICPR
    Automated Whiteboard Lecture Video Summarization by Content Region Detection and Representation
    Bhargava Urala Kota, Alexander Stone, Kenny Davila, and 2 more authors
    In 2020 25th International Conference on Pattern Recognition (ICPR), 2021
    Abs Bib PDF

    Whiteboard lecture videos are summarized by detecting handwritten content regions and comparing their feature representations to find unique content. The method handles occluded, growing, and disappearing handwriting, and produces both key content and keyframes. Weighted spatio-temporal conflict minimization is used to segment the lecture.

2019

  1. IJDAR
    Generalized Framework for Summarization of Fixed-Camera Lecture Videos by Detecting and Binarizing Handwritten Content
    Bhargava Urala Kota, Kenny Davila, Alexander Stone, and 2 more authors
    International Journal on Document Analysis and Recognition (IJDAR), 2019
    Abs Bib PDF

    A framework for extracting and binarizing handwritten content in fixed-camera lecture videos, so the content can be used to index and navigate the video. A deep learning pipeline detects handwritten text, formulae, and sketches, and the spatio-temporal structure of those detections is used to segment the lecture. On the AccessMath dataset the framework reaches an F-measure of 94.32 percent for binary connected components.

  2. ICDARW
    Summarizing Lecture Videos by Key Handwritten Content Regions
    Bhargava Urala Kota, Saleem Ahmed, Alexander Stone, and 3 more authors
    In 2019 International Conference on Document Analysis and Recognition Workshops (ICDARW), 2019
    Best paper award
    Abs Bib PDF

    Whiteboard lecture videos are summarized by key handwritten content regions. A detector finds semantically meaningful groups of handwriting, and a triplet-loss embedding distinguishes unique content across frames. A spatiotemporal index maps each summary region back to where it appears in the video. On AccessMath the method recalls 92.09 percent of unique ground-truth objects.

  3. TechConnect
    Post-Processing Effects on 3D Dynamic Models Created from Additive Manufacturing
    Dennis Fedorishin, Alexander Stone, N. Eadie, and 3 more authors
    In TechConnect Briefs, 2019
    Alexander Stone and Dennis Fedorishin contributed equally
    Abs Bib HTML PDF

    Automated post-processing of 3D-printed dynamic models reduces surface roughness and moving resistance. On a printed engine and gearbox, post-processing cut surface roughness by 38 percent and substantially reduced static and moving resistance.

  4. TechConnect

    In TechConnect Briefs, 2019
    Abs Bib HTML PDF

  1. ICFHR
    Automated Detection of Handwritten Whiteboard Content in Lecture Videos for Summarization
    Bhargava Urala Kota, Kenny Davila, Alexander Stone, and 2 more authors
    In 2018 16th International Conference on Frontiers in Handwriting Recognition (ICFHR), 2018
    Abs Bib PDF

    A scene-text detector is adapted to find handwritten text, math, and sketches on a whiteboard, including content occluded by the lecturer. The detections are used to summarize the lecture. The work also released timestamped bounding-box annotations for the AccessMath dataset.
